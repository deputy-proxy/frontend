Thornhill is a pre-eminent law firm serving a global client base in Private Capital, Private Wealth, M&A and Disputes.

We advise them on their most significant transactions, their most sensitive disputes, and the long-term structuring of their affairs.

We do not organise ourselves strictly along departmental lines. Instead, we bring together lawyers from across the firm to form a team suited to the specific demands of each client and matter, so clients get direct access to the right expertise rather than being passed between disconnected specialists.
