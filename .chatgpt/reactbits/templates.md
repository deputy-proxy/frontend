## Template lessons for the Author

- A template is useful for **composition logic**, not as a mandatory section order.
- Extract the template's visual grammar: density, navigation behavior, hero strategy, media treatment, section transitions, and motion language.
- Combine a template's structural idea with a different design skill when the business context calls for it.
- Do not replicate brand-specific copy, artwork, or source implementation unless the task is explicitly authorized and the assets are legitimately available.
- Use templates as references for page-level coherence. Blocks are better for section-level composition.
