About Arbor

Independent, thoughtful, and grounded in evidence.

Arbor Institute is an independent research and education organization focused on climate resilience, sustainable land use, and practical environmental policy.

Its work is intended for researchers, policymakers, nonprofit leaders, educators, and professionals who need evidence they can examine, discuss, and apply.

Arbor's visual language reflects its intended posture: measured, contemporary, and serious about the relationship between research and the places it seeks to understand.
