Method / 02

Evidence before certainty.

Verification is not a magic truth button. It is a structured way to inspect what supports a claim and what does not.

01

Identify the claim

Separate the actual statement being made from headlines, opinions, marketing language, and surrounding noise.

Claim / scope
02

Trace the evidence

Surface supporting and contradicting sources, with source type and context instead of a mysterious confidence score.

Sources / support
03

Check freshness

A source can be reputable and still be too old to answer a current question. Time belongs in the evidence.

Show uncertainty

Why it matters / 05

The internet is very good at making confidence look like evidence.

A polished page can be wrong. A screenshot can be old. A source can be real and still be irrelevant to the claim being made. Valid.guide is designed around that awkward fact.

NOTE

A verification result should explain the reasoning a person needs to make their own judgment.
