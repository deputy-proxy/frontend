# Afacere Online v2 — Round 1 Context

The supplied brief is the source of truth.

## Required business outcome
A premium Romanian digital platform helping people start, build, and grow online businesses through practical education, tools, guides, and actionable resources.

## Audience
Aspiring entrepreneurs, freelancers, creators, small business owners, and professionals.

## Positioning
**Afacere Online**  
**Construiește ceva care îți aparține.**

Supporting message:
**Practical knowledge for building an online business, from the first idea to consistent revenue.**

## Required taxonomy
Idei · Strategie · Marketing · Vânzări · Sisteme · Finanțe

## Round 1 boundary
Ten independent HTML directions use only typography, flat color, and whitespace. No production framework, imagery, shapes, borders, gradients, animation, SVG, or component system is introduced before an explicit human lock.

## Human judgement
The directions are linked from `index.html`. Concept capsules are kept outside that index so the first encounter is the visual work itself.
