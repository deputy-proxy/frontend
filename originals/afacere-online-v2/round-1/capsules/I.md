# I

- Premise: A compressed catalog: business knowledge is treated like a collection of numbered entries, with scale and indentation doing the navigation.
- Fonts: Georgia for display; Arial for body; Courier New for index.
- Flat canvas: #FAFAF7
- Active text colors: #191919, #6A5B43, #A22E2E
- Signature: Catalog numbering and indentation as the primary navigation system.
- Round 2 invariant: preserve the typographic reading path, hierarchy, whitespace rhythm, and palette roles before introducing production UI.
- Assumptions: no external font dependency; system-available stacks only.
- Risks: display font availability varies by operating system; verify Romanian diacritics in production.
