# C

- Premise: A typographic operating manual: dense, precise, almost technical, with the business areas treated as navigational coordinates.
- Fonts: Arial Narrow/Arial for display; Courier New for metadata.
- Flat canvas: #EDEDED
- Active text colors: #111111
- Signature: Compressed technical headline scale paired with coordinate-like metadata.
- Round 2 invariant: preserve the typographic reading path, hierarchy, whitespace rhythm, and palette roles before introducing production UI.
- Assumptions: no external font dependency; system-available stacks only.
- Risks: display font availability varies by operating system; verify Romanian diacritics in production.
