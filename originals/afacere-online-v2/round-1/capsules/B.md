# B

- Premise: A newspaper-like front page where the platform behaves as an editorial desk rather than a software product.
- Fonts: Georgia for headline; Courier New for metadata; Arial for body.
- Flat canvas: #F7F7F2
- Active text colors: #101010, #B34A2D, #315C4D
- Signature: Newspaper masthead hierarchy and three-column editorial reading.
- Round 2 invariant: preserve the typographic reading path, hierarchy, whitespace rhythm, and palette roles before introducing production UI.
- Assumptions: no external font dependency; system-available stacks only.
- Risks: display font availability varies by operating system; verify Romanian diacritics in production.
