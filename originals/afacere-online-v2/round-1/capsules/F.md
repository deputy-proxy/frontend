# F

- Premise: A magazine cover expanded into a homepage: high-contrast display type, narrow captions, and a reading path that feels printed rather than app-like.
- Fonts: Georgia for display; Arial for body; Courier New for captions.
- Flat canvas: #ECE8DE
- Active text colors: #171717, #A33B2A, #2C5A55
- Signature: Magazine-cover hierarchy expanded into a long-form index.
- Round 2 invariant: preserve the typographic reading path, hierarchy, whitespace rhythm, and palette roles before introducing production UI.
- Assumptions: no external font dependency; system-available stacks only.
- Risks: display font availability varies by operating system; verify Romanian diacritics in production.
